Why ZAKUIKit

Everything you need, nothing you don't

A carefully engineered toolkit that stays out of your way while giving you a complete design system out of the box.

Lightweight

Only ~30KB gzipped. No bloat, no legacy weight. Every byte earns its place in the bundle.

No Dependencies

Pure vanilla HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No frameworks, no build step required. Drop it in and go.

Dark Mode

Built-in theming with light, dark and system modes via the data-theme attribute.

Responsive

Mobile-first by design. Grids, spacing, typography and components respond gracefully at every breakpoint.
